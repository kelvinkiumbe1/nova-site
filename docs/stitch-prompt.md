# Google Stitch prompts — Nova Site Care

Paste the **Main prompt** first (web, desktop). Then use the follow-up prompts one page at a time
in the same project so the style stays consistent. Generate a mobile version of each page after.

---

## Main prompt (Home page + design system)

Design a modern, premium, corporate website for **Nova Site Care**, a professional cleaning company
in Nairobi, Kenya, whose main focus is construction cleaning for construction companies, contractors,
property developers, site managers and property owners.

**Brand & style**
- Colours: deep black (#0B0B0B) and rich metallic gold (#C9A24A), balanced with white and warm
  neutral backgrounds (#FAF7F2, #F1ECE3). Gold used sparingly for accents, buttons, thin lines and icons.
- Elegant, clean, corporate, confident. Not overly decorated, no playful illustrations, no gradients
  except a subtle gold sheen on the logo mark.
- Typography: an elegant serif for headings (e.g. Playfair Display / Cormorant) and a clean
  sans-serif for body text (e.g. Inter / Manrope). Generous whitespace, strong grid, large imagery.
- Photography: high-quality, realistic photos of Black African cleaning professionals in neat black
  uniforms with gold logo detail, working in modern residential homes, offices and construction sites
  (dust removal, window cleaning, floor polishing on newly finished buildings).
- Feeling: an established, premium company that can handle anything from a home clean to a large
  construction project.

**Home page sections**
1. Sticky header: logo "NOVA SITE CARE" (black + gold), nav: Home, Services, About, Projects,
   Why Choose Us, Contact; gold "Request a Quote" button.
2. Hero: full-width photo of a cleaning team on a newly completed modern building, dark overlay.
   Headline: "Construction-Ready. Handover-Perfect." Sub-headline: "Premium construction and property
   cleaning across Nairobi." Buttons: "Request a Quotation" (gold) and "Explore Our Services" (outline).
   Small trust strip: "Fully trained teams · Insured · Nairobi-wide coverage".
3. Short company introduction with a photo and a few key numbers (projects completed, sq. ft cleaned,
   years of experience).
4. Construction cleaning services (featured): 4 elegant cards — Progressive Construction Cleaning,
   Cleaning Between Construction Phases, Post-Construction Cleaning, Final Handover Cleaning.
5. Other services row: Residential, Office & Commercial, Carpet & Sofa, Upholstery, Move-In/Move-Out.
6. Featured projects: before/after image comparison slider plus 3 project cards.
7. Why Choose Us: 4 points with thin gold line icons — Professionalism, Reliability,
   Attention to Detail, Quality of Service.
8. Client testimonials on a black background with gold quote marks.
9. Call-to-action band: "Have a project ready for cleaning?" with quote button and WhatsApp button.
10. Footer (black): contacts, service areas in Nairobi, social media icons, quick links.
- A floating green WhatsApp button fixed bottom-right on every page.

## Follow-up prompts

**Services page**
Using the same design system, create the Our Services page. A hero with the title "Our Services".
A large section for Construction Cleaning (the main focus) with the four stages laid out as a
horizontal timeline: Progressive → Between Phases → Post-Construction → Final Handover, each with
a photo, short description and bullet highlights. Below, a grid of general services: Residential,
Office & Commercial, Carpet & Sofa, Upholstery, Move-In/Move-Out. Each card has a "Get a Quote" link.
End with a quote call-to-action band.

**About Us page**
Same design system. Sections: company background story with a team photo, Mission and Vision side
by side in elegant cards with gold borders, Core Values as an icon row, "What makes Nova different"
as a split layout with a large photo, and a team section with portrait photos.

**Projects / Gallery page**
Same design system. Filter tabs (All, Construction, Commercial, Residential). A masonry gallery of
completed projects, each card with location and service type. A featured before-and-after comparison
slider at the top. A "Our Teams at Work" photo strip at the bottom.

**Why Choose Us page**
Same design system. A bold statement hero, four pillars (Professionalism, Reliability, Attention to
Detail, Quality of Service) each with a photo and short paragraph, a "Our Process" 4-step section
(Site visit → Tailored quotation → Scheduled cleaning → Quality inspection & handover), and testimonials.

**Contact Us page**
Same design system. Two columns: left — contact details (phone, email, WhatsApp, office location,
business hours) and social icons; right — a clean quotation request form with fields: full name,
company (optional), phone, email, service needed (dropdown), property type, site location, project
stage, approximate size, preferred date, message, photo upload, and a gold "Submit Request" button.
Below, a full-width embedded Google Map of Nairobi with a list of service coverage areas.

**Admin dashboard**
Same brand, simplified: a clean admin dashboard with a black sidebar (Dashboard, Quote Requests,
Services, Projects, Gallery, Testimonials, Site Settings) and white content area. Dashboard shows
cards for new quote requests this week, open leads and published projects, and a table of recent
quote requests with status badges (New, Contacted, Quoted, Won, Lost).
